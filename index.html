My Favorite Recipe - Maggi

Maggi is my favorite food. It is easy and quick to make.

Ingredients

  • 1 packet Maggi
  • 1 cup Water
  • 1 Tomato
  • 1 Onion
  • Maggi Masala

Instructions

  1. Take a pan.
  2. Add one cup of water.
  3. Add Maggi noodles.
  4. Add vegetables and masala.
  5. Cook for 2-3 minutes.
  6. Your Maggi is ready!

Enjoy your delicious Maggi!
